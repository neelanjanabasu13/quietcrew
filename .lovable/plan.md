# Outshine: respond to "Businesses in ChatGPT"

## What OpenAI announced (10 Nov 2026, beta)
- Any business, wherever ChatGPT is available (so UK too), can claim its listing in ChatGPT's Business Hub and manage its details.
- Verified businesses see total figures on how often they are mentioned, how many website clicks they get and common topics.
- The "Business Agent" chatbot is for US ecommerce and online service businesses only, so it does not cover UK trades.

## What it means for Outshine
- Good for Outshine: it proves AI visibility is now a real channel, and it gives owners one more profile to claim and keep up to date.
- A risk: owners may think "I claimed it in ChatGPT, so I am done". Claiming fixes the listing itself, but not the public signals (reviews, photos, website, consistent details) that AI tools use to decide who to recommend.
- Outshine cannot read Business Hub data, so we must never say we check it.

## Proposed changes
1. Free report: add a check marked "Unknown, not publicly checkable" called "Claimed in ChatGPT Business Hub", with a one-line tip telling the owner to claim it.
2. Fix Pack: add a step-by-step "Claim your ChatGPT listing" guide that reuses the Fix Pack's business description, so the details match everywhere.
3. Outshine FAQ: add "Do I still need this if I claim my business in ChatGPT?", explaining that claiming helps but recommendations still depend on public signals.
4. Copy: no claims about OpenAI partnerships or about any effect on rankings.

## Technical notes
- New static check entry in the Outshine checks list with status unknown, plus a guide section added to the Fix Pack content and a new FAQ entry (which also updates the FAQ structured data).
- No new Google fields and no extra API cost.
