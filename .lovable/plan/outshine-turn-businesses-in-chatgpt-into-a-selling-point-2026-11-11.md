# Outshine: turn "Businesses in ChatGPT" into a selling point

## Does it cannibalise Outshine?
Only in part. ChatGPT Business Hub is a free place for an owner to claim their listing and see how ChatGPT talks about them, but it only covers ChatGPT and only shows what is already happening. It does not grade the owner's Google profile, compare them with local rivals, write the review replies, description or photo list for them, or cover Google, Gemini and Perplexity. Outshine does those things, and it is built for a busy tradesperson who will never open a dashboard. The real risk is that owners believe claiming the listing is enough, so Outshine should present Business Hub as step one and itself as the part that gets the business recommended.

## How to use it to our advantage
1. Free report: add a check called "Claimed in ChatGPT Business Hub", marked Unknown because we cannot see it, with a tip and a link to claim it. This gives the owner an easy win and builds trust.
2. Fix Pack: add a click-by-click "Claim and fill in your ChatGPT listing" guide that reuses the Fix Pack's business description and services, so the details match on Google, the website and ChatGPT. Business Hub then becomes something the Fix Pack fills in for you.
3. Verification scan: tell owners to note their Business Hub mention and click numbers before they make changes and again afterwards. This gives them proof that the work paid off, which we cannot measure ourselves.
4. Marketing angle on the Outshine page: "ChatGPT now lets you claim your business. Claiming it is the easy part, and being the one it recommends is what Outshine helps with."
5. FAQ: add "Do I still need Outshine if I claim my business in ChatGPT?"
6. Goldmine (later): pitch agencies on "help your clients set up ChatGPT Business Hub properly" as an outreach hook.

## Guardrails
- We never claim to read Business Hub data or to have any link with OpenAI.
- We never promise any effect on rankings. The Business Agent chatbot is US-only, so we do not mention it for UK trades.

## Technical notes
- New check entry in the Outshine checks list with status unknown, a new guide section in the Fix Pack content, a new FAQ entry (which updates the FAQ structured data) and one copy block on the Outshine page.
- No new Google fields and no extra API cost.
